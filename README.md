Emberfall — a top-down arena survival game. You are the last lantern-keeper in an ashen field: burn the ash-wisps with sparks, gather the kindling they drop, chain kills into a combo, and survive 10 waves until dawn breaks. Five enemy kinds (drifting wisps, lunging dashers, splitting orbs, ranged spitters, and the Ashen Herald boss), pickups (kindling, hearts, shield, rapid fire, nova bombs), synthesized WebAudio sound, and a seeded deterministic world — the same seed always plays the same game.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. WASD or arrows to move, mouse to aim, hold click or Space to shoot, B for a nova bomb, P to pause, M to mute. `?seed=N` fixes the spawn pattern; `?shot=start|play|end|win` stages a screenshot scene.
